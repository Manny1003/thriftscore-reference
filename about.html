My story

Why I created ThriftScore

ThriftScore grew from my lifelong connection to secondhand and a problem I kept seeing: an incredible community with no single place built to help it discover, connect, and learn.

My story: How it got started

Secondhand has always been part of my story

What began out of necessity during my childhood grew into a lifelong love of discovering the unique, the unexpected, and the cherished.

My mother and I explored thrift stores, resale shops, outdoor markets, and antique malls, then came home to share our latest scores with my dad. I later continued that tradition with my own children.

  • I grew up with a connection to secondhand that began in childhood
  • Sharing our latest scores became a family tradition
  • That passion stayed with me through travel and everyday life

The problem

The secondhand world was growing, but discovery still felt scattered.

Again and again, I found that discovering the right stores was surprisingly difficult and valuable time was lost. Information was spread across websites, social media groups, outdated directories, and word of mouth.

I also saw that great stores were not always represented well by generic reviews. Their personality, specialties, and purpose could be lost, even though those details are exactly what make a secondhand destination worth visiting.

At the same time, I realized there was no single type of thrift shopper. Some people love digging through bins for hidden treasures. Others seek curated boutiques specializing in luxury brands, vintage fashion, furniture, or collectibles. I wanted the discovery experience to reflect those differences.

I could see that the challenge went beyond shoppers. Independent entrepreneurs, nonprofit organizations, resale businesses, creators, and other members of the secondhand economy were part of a large but disconnected ecosystem. I believed better discovery could mean stronger connections and more visibility for the people and businesses that make this community work.

“I’d love to thrift, but I don’t know where to go.”

I heard this from friends again and again, and it captured the discovery problem in a simple way.

What shaped my idea

A lifelong connection

Secondhand began as a childhood necessity for me and grew into a lifelong love of discovery, creativity, value, and the stories carried by things with a history.

A discovery problem

Across travel and everyday life, I kept finding that the right thrift, vintage, antique, and resale places were surprisingly difficult to discover because useful information lived in too many different places.

A fragmented ecosystem

I realized the challenge extended beyond shoppers. Independent businesses, entrepreneurs, nonprofits, creators, and resale organizations also needed better ways to be discovered and connected.

Experience behind the idea

More than 25 years building and scaling organizations

I have spent more than 25 years as an executive leader and Chief Human Resources Officer, helping high-growth technology companies align strategy, people, culture, and execution. My experience includes rapid growth, international expansion, acquisitions, organizational transformation, and public company readiness.

Why ThriftScore

Bringing two worlds together

ThriftScore brings together my lifelong love of secondhand and my decades of experience building and scaling technology businesses. I founded it with the belief that the independent businesses, entrepreneurs, creators, and shoppers who make this community remarkable can be even stronger when they are easier to discover and better connected.
