About ThriftScore

ThriftScore is one place for the whole secondhand world, a way to discover stores, sales, and events, connect with people who share your passion, plan your thrift crawls, and learn from the community.
Store finders show you pins on a map. Marketplaces sell you things. ThriftScore brings discovery, community, planning, and learning together, and it is built specifically for secondhand — thrift stores, estate sales, antique malls, flea markets, vintage shops, and the people around them.
Shoppers, collectors, vintage lovers, resellers, upcyclers, creators, store owners, estate sale companies, and nonprofits.
